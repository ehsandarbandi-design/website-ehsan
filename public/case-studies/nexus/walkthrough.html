How do loyalty members shop differently?

Nexus

Members who redeem rewards buy 1.8× more often, and lapse risk rises sharply after 45 days without a purchase, so a timely win-back journey is the quickest way to protect volume.

The segments worth prioritizing:

  • Everyday Loyalists: frequent buyers of core brands at home and at work
  • Flavor Explorers: the fastest-growing segment at +12% volume
  • Value Stockers: family shoppers buying multipacks in grocery
